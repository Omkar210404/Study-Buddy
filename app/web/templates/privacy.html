Privacy

Your notes are yours. This page says exactly where they go.

Right now: {% include "_mode_line.html" %}

What is stored

Everything is kept in one SQLite database file on this computer ({{ settings.database_path }}): the text of your notes, the passages, your cards, and your review and quiz history. Uploaded files are read once and not kept. There are no accounts and no analytics, and the pages load no fonts or scripts from other sites.

Offline mode

This is the default. Importing notes, generating cards, reviewing, quizzes, the progress page and the Anki export all run on this computer. Nothing leaves the machine.

LLM mode

LLM mode is optional and off unless you set it up with environment variables. When you press "Generate cards with the model" on a set of notes:

  • Sent to the provider you configured: the text of the passages being processed in that run (at most {{ settings.llm_max_passages_per_run }} per run), each with its section title and the instructions for writing cards.
  • Never sent: your other notes and decks, your existing cards, your review history, file names, or anything about you.

What the provider does with the passages is governed by that provider's own terms, so do not use LLM mode for notes you would not share with them. Your API key is read from the environment, is used only in the request header to that provider, and is never stored in the database or shown on any page.

Deleting your data

Delete a deck from its page to remove its notes, cards and history. To remove everything, close the app and delete the database file.
